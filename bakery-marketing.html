Bakery marketing: the honest playbook.

A bakery has the easiest content in food and the hardest math: everything you make expires today. Marketing's job is to sell the case out by close and stack tomorrow with pre-orders — beautiful photos are just the means.

The 6am advantage

Nothing on the internet outperforms fresh bread at dawn. Shoot it when it comes out — steam, crumb shots, the tray of croissants — and post before your town starts deciding where to stop. Same-morning posting is the trick: “these exist right now” converts in a way that a stock pastry photo never will. Load your best to the Google Business Profile too; that's what a stranger sees comparing “bakery near me” options.

Orders are the growth lever

Walk-in traffic has a ceiling; pre-orders and custom work don't. Cakes, catering trays, weekly bread subscriptions, holiday pre-sales — that's revenue you bake to order instead of guessing. Make ordering stupidly easy: a form or even a dedicated phone hour beats “DM us.” Holiday pre-order campaigns started three weeks early routinely become a bakery's biggest revenue weeks of the year; plan them like the events they are.

The foundation and what to skip

Hours accurate everywhere (sell-out early? say so — scarcity is charming when it's honest), menu and prices in readable text, allergen and custom-order info on the site because those exact questions get asked to AI assistants. Reviews answered warmly. What to skip: discounting day-olds into a habit that trains customers to wait, and buying followers or ads before the organic machine — morning posts, profile, pre-orders — is running. A bakery's product photographs itself; spend effort, not money.

Questions people actually ask

How do bakeries attract more customers?

Same-morning photos where locals actually look — Google profile and social — plus accurate hours and reviews. The comparison for “bakery near me” is won by whoever looks freshest and most alive today, not by the biggest ad budget.

What should a bakery post on social media?

This morning's bake, posted this morning. Process video — lamination, decorating, the oven pull — performs for free. Two to five real posts a week; the feed should smell like the shop.

How can a bakery increase sales without more foot traffic?

Pre-orders and subscriptions: cakes, catering, holiday boxes, weekly bread. Baked-to-order revenue has no waste and no ceiling, and a simple order form converts followers into customers without them ever standing in line.

Should a bakery take custom cake orders through Instagram DMs?

Take the inquiry anywhere, but move it to a form or email fast — DMs lose details, deposits, and dates. A simple order form with flavors, sizes, lead time, and deposit terms protects both sides and looks more professional.
