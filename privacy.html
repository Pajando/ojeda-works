Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Who we are

Ojeda Works is a sole-proprietor business in Napa, California, operated by Alejandro Ojeda. Contact: alejandro@ojedaworks.com.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and sets no cookies of its own. If you email us, we receive what you send — your address and message — and use it only to reply and work with you. We don't sell personal information, and we don't buy contact lists.

The custom songs page has an order form. What you type into it (like your name, email, and the story for your song) is delivered to our email by FormSubmit, and we use it only to make your song and talk with you about it.

Hosting and third-party requests

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs standard technical data (like IP addresses) per GitHub's privacy policy. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and CDNFonts, and a graphics library from jsDelivr; those services receive the standard technical request data any web asset does. We don't control or receive that data.

Instagram

We use an app we built ourselves, "Ojeda Works Poster," connected through Meta's official Instagram API. It is connected only to our own Instagram account, @ojedaworks. It is not offered to anyone else, and it never logs into anyone else's account.

The app is used to: publish our own posts; read the performance numbers for our own posts (like reach, views and saves); and read comments and direct messages sent to @ojedaworks, so we can answer them. We also use it to send replies. Replies may be written by a person or sent automatically, as explained below.

We also run a message tool (FewChat) on our own Cloudflare account. It saves direct messages to and from @ojedaworks (which can include earlier conversations and group chats), comments on our posts that Instagram sends to it, Instagram IDs, usernames, names and profile picture links, and message details such as reactions, attachments and whether a message we sent was opened or a button in it was tapped. Voice notes and videos sent to us may be turned into text by Cloudflare's AI service. Some replies may be sent automatically, for example when you comment a specific word on a post, and can include a public reply under your comment. AI-written automatic replies are turned off. We also keep post numbers in a spreadsheet on our own computer.

Your messages also remain in Instagram. Cloudflare stores the saved data and does that transcription for us. We don't sell this information or use it for advertising, and we don't share it with others unless the law or Meta's terms require it. The tool's saved copies are kept until you ask us to delete them or we shut it down.

Deleting your data

If you commented on or messaged @ojedaworks and want us to delete anything we've saved about you, email alejandro@ojedaworks.com with your Instagram username. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. This does not delete anything inside Instagram, and new activity can be saved again. You can delete your own comments and messages in Instagram, but deleting a comment there does not remove our saved copy, so email us.

Analytics

We currently run no analytics on this site. If that changes (for example, adding Google Analytics), this policy will be updated first to say exactly what's measured.

Your rights

California residents may have rights under the CCPA, including knowing what personal information a business holds and requesting deletion. To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, email us.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed at children under 13; we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. No silent edits.
