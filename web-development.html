Website Development

A site AI can actually read is a site that can become the answer.

A page Google or an AI model can't understand clearly can't be recommended. Clean, fast, schema-marked sites built so a visitor and an AI model both know exactly what a business does — and why it's the answer.

Talk about a site

What this covers

  • New builds — a site from nothing, structured around what customers actually search for.
  • Rebuilds — taking a slow or outdated site and making it fast, clean, and current.
  • Schema & structured data — the JSON-LD markup that lets Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity read a page correctly instead of guessing.
  • Indexing & visibility work — sitemap setup, Search Console, and getting pages actually found, not just published.

How a project runs

  • 1. Map what customers search for — real queries, not guesses, before a single page gets planned.
  • 2. Structure the site around that — pages that match how people actually look for the business, not a generic template.
  • 3. Build it fast and clean — static where possible, no bloated plugins slowing down every page load.
  • 4. Mark it up and submit it — schema, sitemap, and Search Console, so the site gets found instead of sitting unindexed.
The proof
