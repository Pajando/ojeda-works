Website Development

Customers find your site and leave without calling? Website development that gets you the call.

If your site is slow, confusing on a phone, or unclear about what you do, some customers leave and call someone else. I build clean, fast sites so a visitor and Google both know what your business does and why to pick you. Google says it uses structured data to understand what a page is about.

Talk about a site

What this covers

  • New builds: a site from scratch, built around what your customers actually search for.
  • Rebuilds: taking a slow or outdated site and making it fast, clean, and current.
  • Schema & structured data: behind-the-scenes code that spells out your name, hours, services and location. Google uses it to understand a page. It does not guarantee a ranking.
  • Indexing & visibility work: sitemap setup, Google Search Console, and asking Google to index new pages, not just publishing them.

How a project runs

  • 1. Map what customers search for: real searches, not guesses, before a single page gets planned.
  • 2. Build the pages around that: pages that match how people actually look for your business, not a generic template.
  • 3. Build it fast and clean: static where possible, no heavy plugins slowing down every page.
  • 4. Mark it up and submit it: schema, sitemap, and Search Console, so Google knows the pages exist.
