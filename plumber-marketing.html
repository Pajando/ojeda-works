Plumber marketing: the honest playbook.

Plumbing is the most urgent-intent trade there is — when the water's coming, the search is now and the first trustworthy answer wins. Marketing a plumbing business means being that answer. Here's how.

Win the flooded-kitchen search

“Plumber near me” at 9pm goes to whoever looks real and answers: profile complete with emergency availability stated honestly, phones actually answered (missed calls are the silent leak in plumbing marketing — a call service pays for itself), reviews recent and plentiful, service area explicit. If you offer 24/7, say it everywhere; if you don't, honest hours beat a broken promise.

Review velocity matters more here

Urgent searchers read fast and recent — five reviews from last month beat fifty from three years ago. Build the ask into the job close: fixed, tested, happy customer, one-tap link, their own words with the job and town. Drain cleared in [town] this week is the strongest ad a plumber can run, and it's free.

Projects, maintenance, and the lead math

Beyond emergencies: water heaters, repipes, and remodel work are researched purchases — service pages with straight talk on options and honest ranges win them. Maintenance plans (annual water-heater flush, inspection) turn one crisis customer into a relationship. Lead platforms are famously aggressive in plumbing: shared leads, bid wars, real per-lead prices — track cost per booked job with cold honesty before renewing anything.

Questions people actually ask

How do plumbers get more emergency calls?

Be findable, credible, and reachable at the panic moment: complete profile with availability stated, recent reviews, and answered phones. The plumber who picks up wins jobs from the three who didn't — responsiveness is the marketing.

Are Google ads worth it for plumbers?

Emergency terms carry real intent and real per-click prices in this trade — worth testing small with tight service-area caps, measured on booked jobs. Long-term the same searches convert free through profile and reviews; ads buy position while the foundation grows.

How do plumbers get reviews fast?

Ask at the moment of relief — fixed and tested — with a one-tap link, letting customers use their own words. Make it a habit on every job; velocity compounds. Never buy or fake reviews: platforms catch it, and in a trust trade the penalty is fatal.

Should a plumber offer maintenance plans?

Yes — inspections, flushes, and priority-response plans smooth revenue and keep you the household's plumber before the next crisis shops around. The marketing benefit is the relationship: plan customers don't search when the pipe bursts; they call you.
