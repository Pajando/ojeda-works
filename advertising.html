Talk about a campaign

How this is approached

  • Start with the offer — a campaign only runs once there's something specific and true to say.
  • Match the platform to the audience — where the actual customers are, not wherever is trendiest.
  • Track what it costs to get a real result — not just clicks or impressions, but the number that matters: a lead, a call, a sale.
  • Cut what isn't working — fast, instead of letting a bad campaign run out the budget.

How a campaign would run

  • 1. Find the real offer — the specific thing worth paying to put in front of people, not a vague "check us out."
  • 2. Pick the platform on evidence — where the actual customers already are, checked, not assumed.
  • 3. Launch small and watch the number that matters — a lead, a call, a sale, not a vanity metric.
  • 4. Scale what works, cut what doesn't — fast, before a bad campaign eats the budget.
