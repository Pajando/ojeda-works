About

Built so you're the answer, not an also-ran.

Every division of Ojeda Works — AO Audit, video, web, content, advertising — exists for the same reason: to make a business the answer someone gets, not one option out of ten. One name, put on the work directly, instead of hidden behind a made-up agency word.

It started with AO Audit

AO Audit came first — a tool built to check whether AI models like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews actually recommend a business, and to fix what's stopping them. It's a real, live product doing real work for businesses in Napa County.

Building it took video, web development, written content, and eventually advertising — the same set of skills most small businesses need for anything they build. It made sense to put one name over all of it, instead of treating each piece as a separate, disconnected service.

Why the name is Ojeda Works

The name went through a long list of invented words and abstract brand names before landing on the obvious answer: a surname, plus what it actually does. No studio, no agency, no media — those words describe a category, not a person. "Ojeda Works" describes exactly what it is: work, done by Ojeda, with a name on it that doesn't change depending on what sounds trendier.

Who this is for

Small businesses, mostly in Napa County, who need real video, web, content, or advertising work and either can't afford a full agency or don't want one — the twelve-person roster, the account manager layer, the retainer that outlasts the results.

What Ojeda Works won't do

No fake reviews. No fabricated credentials. No growth-hack promise that can't be backed up. The businesses getting hurt right now are the ones being sold certainty nobody actually has — that's not how this works, even when it would be an easier sell.

How it works day to day

One person does the work across every division — video, web, content, and advertising. That means slower scale than an agency with twelve people, and it also means no handoffs, no account manager translating between a client and the person actually doing the work, and no fabricated case studies to cover for a team that didn't do it themselves.
