Questions people actually ask

How do I get my Google review link?

Open your Google Business Profile (business.google.com or the Google Maps app as the owner), find “Ask for reviews,” and copy the share link — it usually starts with g.page. Paste that link here and the QR code will point customers straight to the review form.

Is it allowed to ask customers for Google reviews with a QR code?

Yes — Google's own guidance suggests sharing your review link or a QR code. What's prohibited is offering money, discounts, or freebies in exchange for reviews, and filtering so only happy customers get asked.

Is this QR generator really free? What's the catch?

Really free, no catch: no email, no signup, no watermark, and your link is never sent anywhere — the code is generated by a script running in your own browser. We make websites and marketing for small businesses; the tool is the handshake.

Where should I put the QR poster?

Wherever the happy moment happens: the counter, the table tent, the invoice clipboard, the back of the receipt, the job-done photo you text over. Pair it with an actual ask — “would you scan this and tell people what we did?” — and it works several times harder.
