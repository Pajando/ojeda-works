Questions people actually ask

How much does a custom song cost?

It depends on length, language, and how much production the style needs — so the process starts with your story, and you get the exact price and timeline in the reply, before anything is made. No subscriptions, no surprise fees.

How is the song actually made?

The lyrics are written from the details you send — real names, real moments — then the track is produced with modern AI music tools and shaped, reviewed, and finished by a person. You get a finished, listenable song, not a robotic template with a name inserted.

Can the song be in Spanish?

Yes — Spanish, English, or both in one song. Corridos, rancheras, baladas, banda, cumbia, country, pop: written naturally in the language you choose by someone who actually speaks it, not translated by software.

What do I need to provide?

Just the form below: who it's for, the occasion, the style if you have one, and the story — names, memories, inside jokes, the phrase they always say. The more real detail you give, the more the song sounds like it could only be about them.

What do I receive, and can I share it?

A finished audio track delivered by email — yours to keep, play at the event, and share with the person it was made for. If you want it for business use, like an ad or a jingle, say so in the form and it gets handled accordingly.
