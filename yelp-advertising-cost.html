Advertising

Yelp keeps calling about ads? Yelp advertising cost, the honest breakdown.

The free Yelp listing is worth claiming. The paid ads start at $150 a month on Yelp's own pricing page, and they deserve harder questions than a sales call invites. Both halves, honestly.

The free listing: claim it regardless

A claimed, complete Yelp profile costs nothing. My take: it can matter beyond Yelp too, since other sites and AI tools can pull from Yelp when they describe a business. Photos, hours, services and honest replies to reviews: that part is pure upside, no purchase needed.

What the paid ads cost

Yelp's local business pricing page lists Yelp Ads “from $150/month” and Yelp Ads plus its Upgrade Package “from $270/month,” both marked “Cancel anytime,” and notes that minimum budgets can change (checked October 2026). The ads buy you spots in Yelp search, targeted by location and keyword. The Upgrade Package also keeps competitor ads off your own page. My take: whether it pays depends on your category and city. It can work where people already search Yelp for what you sell, and it won't where your customers don't use Yelp.

The questions to ask before signing

Ask what a click costs and what you can cap it at. Ask for results from your category and city, not national averages. Ask how you cancel, and get it in writing. And run a free test before any pitch: ask your last ten customers whether they've ever hired anyone through Yelp. If none have, you have your answer.

Questions people actually ask

Is Yelp advertising worth it for a small business?

It depends on your category. My take: where customers already search Yelp, like restaurants and some home services in some cities, it can pay. Where they don't, no ad budget fixes that. Ask your customers how they found you, and advertise where their answers point.

Why does Yelp keep calling me about ads?

My take: selling ads by phone is part of how Yelp grows. You're allowed to keep the free listing and say no to ads. If a rep offers free credit, ask what it turns into and when billing starts.

Do Yelp reviews affect my Google and AI visibility?

They can. My take: Yelp is one of the places search engines and AI tools can read about a business, so keep the free profile accurate even if you never buy ads. Use the same name, address, phone and hours everywhere.

Can I turn off Yelp ads anytime?

Yelp's local business pricing page says “Cancel anytime” for the plans it lists there. If a sales rep offers a different package or a contract, read the terms before signing and get the cancellation steps in writing on day one.
