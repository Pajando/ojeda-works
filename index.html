Questions people actually ask

What is Ojeda Works?

Ojeda Works is the parent brand over AO Audit and everything else built here — video, website development, content and social, and advertising. One person does the work across all of it, so nothing gets lost between departments.

Why does Ojeda Works exist?

Because most small businesses get handed off between an agency's account manager, a junior designer, and a freelancer who never talks to either — and the work suffers for it. Ojeda Works exists so the person who understands your business is the same person building for it, from the first video to the last line of code.

What's different about working with one person instead of an agency?

No handoffs, no waiting on a chain of approvals, and no explaining your business twice. What you get is direct answers, faster turnaround, and work that stays consistent because one person is holding the whole picture in their head.

What's the relationship between Ojeda Works and AO Audit?

AO Audit came first — a tool that checks whether AI models like ChatGPT actually recommend a business, and fixes what's stopping them. It's the proof this whole approach works. Ojeda Works is the name over AO Audit and everything built after it.

Does Ojeda Works use fake reviews or fabricated stats?

No. No fabricated reviews, no invented numbers, no borrowed credentials. If a claim is on this site, it's true and it's checkable — that rule doesn't bend for a better-sounding sentence.
