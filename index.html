Questions people actually ask

What is Ojeda Works?

You get one place for the things that bring customers in: video, website development, content and social, and advertising. One person does all of it. It also includes a free check of whether Google and AI can read your website.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who says yes. Google's own help page says: "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google." Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and how well-known a business is. Nobody can promise you a spot. What you get is the work that gives you a real chance: a clear site, an accurate Google profile and content that answers what customers ask.

Source: Google Business Profile Help, "How to improve your local ranking on Google"

What's the relationship between Ojeda Works and the Answer Optimization Audit?

The audit is how you find out if AI recommends your business, and what's stopping it. It came first and it's live. Ojeda Works is the name over the audit and the video, web, content and ads that fix what it finds.

Does Ojeda Works use fake reviews or fabricated stats?

No. You get no fabricated reviews, no invented numbers, no borrowed credentials. If a claim is on this site, it's true and you can check it. That rule doesn't bend for a better-sounding sentence.

What's different about working with one person instead of an agency?

You don't wait on a chain of approvals, and you don't explain your business twice. In my experience, that means direct answers and faster turnaround, and your work stays consistent because one person holds the whole picture. Many small businesses get passed between an account manager, a junior designer and a freelancer who never talk to each other, and the work suffers. Here, the person who understands your business is the person building for it.
