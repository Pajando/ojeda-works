Questions people actually ask

How much does a website cost for a small business?

Typical ranges: DIY platforms $0–$50 a month plus your hours; a freelancer or small studio from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars; agencies from several thousand to $50,000+. What you're buying at each tier is structure — whether the site answers what customers actually search.

Why is the range so wide at each tier?

Because page count, copywriting, bilingual versions, booking systems, and — above all — whether the site is built to rank move the price more than the label 'freelancer' or 'agency' does. The calculator's add-ons show exactly how each choice moves the band.

Does this calculator send my answers anywhere?

No — it's a small script running entirely in your browser. Nothing you select is transmitted or stored. It exists so you walk into any quote conversation already knowing the honest band.

Is a cheap website worth it?

A cheap site that answers real questions beats an expensive one that doesn't. The trap isn't the price — it's paying any amount for a site with no structure for search. Ask every builder how the site will rank for what customers type; the silence is informative.
