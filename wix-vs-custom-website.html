Paying Wix every month and wondering if it's worth it? Wix vs a custom website, the honest comparison.

Wix is the right call for some businesses. This isn't a hit piece. But there's a part the ads skip: what happens to your site when you want to leave. Here's the honest list, both ways.

Where Wix genuinely wins

Fast to launch, and you don't need anyone technical: you can have something online today and edit it yourself. For a brand-new business testing whether an idea has customers at all, that's real value. Wix's plans page lists paid plans from $17.77 to $159.77 a month when you pay for a year up front (checked October 2026). That's cheaper up front than paying someone to build a site.

Where custom wins

Speed, structure, and being easy to read. My take: builder sites often carry extra code that can slow pages down, and templates limit how cleanly search engines and AI tools can read what your business does. A custom static site can be built around what your customers actually search, load fast, include proper schema markup, and cost very little to host after the build. The five-year math at the yearly prices on Wix's plans page: Light ($17.77 a month) comes to about $1,066, and Business ($39.77 a month) to about $2,386. My take: that's in the range of what a simple custom site can cost to build once, and then you own it.

The lock-in question the ads skip

You can't take a Wix site with you. Wix's own help center says your site must run on Wix's servers and can't be hosted somewhere else. Wix also says the content you build there belongs to you, so you can copy your text and images out. But if you leave, you rebuild the pages and the structure. That's the real price of the easy start, and it's fine to pay it knowingly. The mistake is finding out three years in, when the site that was cheap to start has become expensive to leave.

Questions people actually ask

Is Wix good enough for a small business?

For getting online fast and cheap while you prove the business, yes. My take on the tradeoffs: pages can be slower, templates can be harder for search engines to read, and you can't move the site to another host. Good enough to start is real. The question is what you move to once being found starts to matter.

Do custom websites rank better than Wix sites?

Not automatically. My take: it's not the brand name. Custom builds are often faster, built around real searches, and carry cleaner markup for search engines to read. A careful Wix site can beat a lazy custom one. The platform just limits how far you can tune it.

Can I move my Wix site to another platform later?

Not really. Wix's help center says a Wix site must run on Wix's servers and can't be hosted somewhere else. You can copy out your text and images, but the design and setup stay behind. Leaving means rebuilding, so think five years ahead, not just this month.

What does a custom website cost compared to Wix?

Wix's plans page lists paid plans from $17.77 to $159.77 a month when you pay for a year up front (checked October 2026), for as long as the site is up. My take: a simple custom static site usually costs a one-time build fee in the range of a few years of that subscription, then very little to host. Cheap now versus owning it outright is the real comparison.
