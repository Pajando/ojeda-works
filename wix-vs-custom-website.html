Wix vs a custom website: the honest comparison.

Wix is genuinely the right call for some businesses — this isn't a hit piece. But the comparison has a part the ads skip: what happens to your site when you want to leave. Here's the honest ledger, both directions.

Where Wix genuinely wins

Speed to launch and zero technical dependence: you can have something online today and edit it yourself forever. For a brand-new business testing whether an idea has customers at all, that's real value — commonly $17–$40+ a month for the business tiers, cheaper than paying anyone.

Where custom wins

Speed, structure, and readability. Builder sites carry heavy code that loads slower, and their templates limit how cleanly search engines and AI models can read what your business actually does. A custom static site is built around your customers' real searches, loads fast, carries proper schema markup, and costs near zero to run after the build. The five-year math: a builder subscription commonly totals $1,000–$2,400+, roughly the cost of having a simple site built once — which you then own outright.

The lock-in question nobody mentions

You can't take a Wix site with you. Leave, and you rebuild from scratch — the pages, the structure, everything except what you copy-paste out. That's the real price of the easy start, and it's fine to pay it knowingly. The mistake is discovering it three years in, when the site that was cheap to start has become expensive to leave.

Questions people actually ask

Is Wix good enough for a small business?

For getting online fast and cheap while you prove the business — genuinely yes. The tradeoffs are slower pages, template structure that machines read poorly, and lock-in. Good enough to start is real; the question is what you move to once being found starts mattering.

Why do custom websites rank better than Wix sites?

It's not the brand name — Google doesn't penalize Wix. It's that custom builds are typically faster, structured around real search queries, and carry cleaner markup for machines to read. A thoughtfully built Wix site can beat a lazy custom one; the platform just sets the ceiling lower.

Can I move my Wix site to another platform later?

Not really — you can export little beyond your text and images. The structure, design, and setup stay behind. Leaving means rebuilding, which is why the decision deserves five-year thinking instead of this-month thinking.

What does a custom website cost compared to Wix?

Wix business tiers commonly run $17–$40+ monthly, forever. A simple custom static site commonly costs a one-time build fee — comparable to a few years of subscription — then nearly nothing to run. Cheap-now versus own-it-outright is the actual comparison.
