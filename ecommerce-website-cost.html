Store fees eating your profit? Ecommerce website cost, with the real math.

Monthly store fees eating the profit on every sale? Here's what a platform store, a custom build and a lighter payment-link setup really cost, with the prices linked so you can check them.

The platform route

Shopify lists plans from $39 a month (Basic, billed monthly, or $29 a month billed yearly) up to $399 a month (Advanced, billed monthly), and online card payments on Basic cost 2.9% + 30¢ each (Shopify pricing, checked October 2026). Paid apps and a paid theme can add more. My take: it's the right call for a real product catalog, because inventory, shipping and taxes are built in. Check the app bill every few months.

Marketplace-first (Etsy, Amazon) charges listing and sales fees; check each marketplace's own fee page before you list. My take: it's a fair way to test demand before you build anything.

The custom route

My take, an estimate and not a survey: a custom store usually starts at several thousand dollars and climbs. It earns its cost at scale or with unusual needs; for a small catalog it's usually paying for flexibility you won't use.

The lighter path for a small catalog

If you sell a handful of products or services, you may not need a store at all. A simple fast website with payment links can handle checkout. Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per successful US card payment and says it has no setup or monthly fees (Stripe pricing, checked October 2026). On Shopify Basic you pay that same 2.9% + 30¢ online card rate plus the monthly plan, so with a few products the payment-link route skips the plan fee. My take: stay lean until inventory, shipping rules or order volume make a full store worth it.

Questions people actually ask

What's the cheapest way to sell online?

Often payment links on a simple fast website. Stripe lists no setup or monthly fees, just 2.9% plus 30 cents per successful US card payment, checked October 2026. My take: move to a full store platform when inventory, shipping rules or order volume really need it, not before.

Why does my Shopify bill keep growing?

Often apps. The plan is the cost you see; paid apps for reviews, email or bundles can each add a monthly fee. My take: check the app bill every few months and delete what you don't use.

Do ecommerce sites rank on Google differently?

Nobody can promise a ranking. My take: write your own product descriptions instead of pasting the maker's text that other stores also use, and add product details search engines can read, like price and availability. That gives each page a reason to exist.

Should I sell on my own site or a marketplace?

Marketplaces bring traffic and take commission plus the customer relationship; your own site keeps both but must earn its traffic. Testing demand: marketplace. Building a brand: your site. Running both on purpose is a fair plan too.
