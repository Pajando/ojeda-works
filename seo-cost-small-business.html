How much does SEO cost for a small business?

Commonly $500–$5,000 a month for retainers — and an honest breakdown shows much of what small businesses need isn't a retainer at all. Plus the layer most SEO packages don't touch: whether AI recommends you.

What SEO retainers commonly cost

Monthly SEO commonly runs $500–$5,000+ depending on market and scope. Some of that buys real work: content, technical fixes, local optimization. Some buys reports about rankings for keywords nobody searches. The question that cuts through any pitch: “which specific queries will I rank for, and how do you know people search them?” Evidence-based answers are rare — insist on them.

The one-time work that does most of the lifting

For local small businesses, the highest-return SEO is largely one-time: a complete Google Business Profile, a site structured around verified real queries, fast pages with schema markup, consistent listings, and honest reviews. That's project work, not a permanent retainer. Ongoing SEO earns its fee when there's ongoing content worth making — not as a subscription to vague ‘optimization.’

The layer most SEO packages skip

Customers increasingly ask ChatGPT and Google's AI who to hire — and those systems recommend businesses they can read and verify. Traditional SEO packages rarely check this at all. That's exactly what AO Audit was built for: it scores how visible your business is to AI engines and shows what's blocking you. Classic SEO and AI visibility overlap but aren't the same — buying the first without checking the second is optimizing for last year's search.

Questions people actually ask

Is monthly SEO worth it for a small business?

It's worth it when it funds real ongoing work — new content targeting verified queries, or active local competition. It's not worth it as a subscription to reports. Many small businesses do better buying the one-time foundation properly, then reinvesting only when there's real work to do.

Why do SEO prices vary so much?

Because “SEO” spans everything from a profile cleanup to a content war in a brutal market. Price tracks market competitiveness and how much genuine work happens monthly. A $500 retainer in a small market can be honest; a $5,000 one can be theater. Judge the work list, not the number.

How long does SEO take to work?

Foundation fixes (profile, indexing, structure) commonly show movement within weeks; content and reputation compound over months. Anyone promising a #1 ranking on a schedule is selling certainty that doesn't exist — the honest answer is direction and momentum, not dates.

What's the difference between SEO and AI visibility?

SEO targets ranked lists of links; AI visibility targets being the recommendation when someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI directly. They share foundations — readable sites, real reviews, consistent data — but AI adds its own checks. AO Audit measures the AI side specifically.
